# External Message

Email or support reply to customers, end users, partners, vendors, or the public. Support/status-style only — not marketing or press.

## Output

- No internal team, service, squad, tool, ticket, release-train, or process names ("Our backend / Platform team / Jira ticket…", "As per our internal policy…") — describe from the reader's perspective in plain language.
- Never commit to unconfirmed dates, features, or fixes; use conditional language ("We are reviewing this and will follow up when we have a confirmed update.").
- Legal-safe: no admission of fault or negligence, no liability-implying language, no absolute guarantees ("guaranteed", "never happens again"), no blame on the reader, a partner, or another vendor.
- Acknowledge the reader's situation first ("Thank you for reaching out about…") → status, separating known from under review → next steps without invented timelines → contact/follow-up path.
- Short paragraphs; no internal process detail that doesn't help the reader act; no marketing enthusiasm or filler ("Hope that helps!").

## Example

Before: `Sorry our Identity service broke SSO. The Platform squad is looking at AUTH-4421 and we guarantee a fix by Friday so you can keep integrating.`

After: `Thank you for reporting the sign-in issue. We understand this is blocking your integration work. We have confirmed the problem on our side and are actively investigating. We will follow up as soon as we have a confirmed update and next steps. If you need anything further in the meantime, reply to this message.`

# External Message

Email or support reply to customers, end users, partners, vendors, or the public. Support/status-style only — not marketing or press.

## Output

- No internal team, service, squad, tool, ticket, release-train, or process names — describe from the reader's perspective in plain language.
- Never commit to unconfirmed dates, features, or fixes; use conditional language ("We are reviewing this and will follow up when we have a confirmed update.").
- Legal-safe: no admission of fault or negligence, no liability-implying language, no absolute guarantees ("guaranteed", "never happens again"), no blame on the reader, a partner, or another vendor.
- Acknowledge the reader's situation first → status (known vs under review) → next steps without invented timelines → contact/follow-up path.
- Short paragraphs; no internal process detail that doesn't help the reader act; no marketing enthusiasm or filler.
